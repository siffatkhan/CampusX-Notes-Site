Lecture notes from multiple CampusX playlists, turned into something you'd actually want to study from — diagrams for architectures you'd otherwise have to imagine, live widgets for the math that only makes sense once you can nudge a slider, and a quiz at the end of every topic so you know it stuck.

Why this site exists

The source material is dense, topic-organized notes compiled from CampusX's playlists — every definition, formula, and worked example from the videos, exactly as written. Nothing here is shortened or dropped; it's the same content, reformatted for the web with proper math rendering, syntax-highlighted code, and visuals a document can't support. Where a playlist's own notes ran out before the videos did, the remaining topics were written from scratch to keep the track complete — always disclosed on the page.

Every diagram, chart, and interactive widget you see is generated for this site as a learning aid — clearly marked, never presented as part of the original notes.
